# Vision
# Postgres Performance Guardrail Platform

## 1. Mission

Make Postgres performance changes safe, provable, and governable for engineering teams.

---

## 2. Problem We Solve

Teams face repeated performance incidents because they lack:

1. Continuous detection of high-impact query regressions
2. Trusted validation of proposed fixes
3. Guardrails for risky changes
4. Audit-ready decision history

---

## 3. Why Existing Tools Are Not Enough

1. Manual database tools provide control, not decision automation.
2. Stateless AI assistants provide ideas, not evidence or governance.
3. Generic monitoring tools provide telemetry, but often lack actionable, policy-gated workflows.

---

## 4. Product Vision

A control plane that:

1. Detects performance risk continuously
2. Recommends improvements with evidence
3. Validates safely before rollout
4. Enforces policy and approvals
5. Preserves immutable audit trails

---

## 5. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

1. Engineering teams running Postgres in production
2. Fast-moving startups and scale-ups with recurring DB performance issues
3. Platform/DBA teams needing safety and accountability across environments

---

## 6. Core Value Proposition

**We do not just suggest changes. We prevent bad changes and prove good ones.**

Outcomes:

1. Lower incident risk from database performance changes
2. Faster and higher-confidence optimization decisions
3. Clear governance evidence for approvals and reviews

---

## 7. Strategic Pillars

1. **Evidence-first optimization**
2. **Policy-first control**
3. **Human-in-the-loop governance**
4. **Audit-grade traceability**
5. **Tenant-safe operations**

---

## 8. Explicit Non-Goals

1. Becoming a full database admin console replacement
2. Becoming a legal compliance certification platform
3. Running unsafe autonomous production changes without guardrails

---

## 9. Success Definition

The product succeeds when customers can answer:

1. What changed?
2. Why was it approved?
3. What evidence proved it helped?
4. Who decided and when?
